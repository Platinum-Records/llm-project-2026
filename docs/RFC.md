# RFC: Transylvanian Folk & Rap Dual-Mode Creative Agent## 1. Problem DefinitionGeneral-purpose Large Language Models (LLMs) can generate fluent Romanian text, but producing natural and culturally authentic regional creative writing remains challenging. In particular, Transylvanian expressions, local idioms, traditional folk styles, and regional humor may be used inconsistently or without sufficient cultural context.Our project aims to address this limitation by developing a creative agent that transforms ordinary user prompts into either **Transylvanian folk poetry ("*poezie ardelenească*") or regional hip-hop lyrics ("*rap ardelenesc*")**. The system will focus on preserving the meaning of the original prompt while incorporating appropriate regional vocabulary, cultural references, rhyme patterns, and rhythmic structures.## 2. State of the ArtExisting solutions for creative text generation can be divided into two main categories.**General-purpose LLMs**, such as ChatGPT and Claude, offer flexibility, strong contextual understanding, and fluent text generation. However, achieving consistent regional authenticity, natural dialect usage, and specific poetic structures may require detailed prompting and repeated revisions.**Rule-based and template-based generators** can enforce predefined structures, vocabulary choices, and rhyme patterns. They are relatively predictable and straightforward to validate, but often produce repetitive results and struggle to adapt creatively to unfamiliar topics or complex instructions.These approaches highlight a gap between flexible text generation and reliable control over regional language and poetic structure. Our project aims to combine the strengths of both approaches while reducing their respective limitations.## 3. ProposalWe propose a **dual-mode creative agent** based on a Small Language Model (SLM), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server providing specialized text-analysis tools.Unlike a conventional chatbot that relies primarily on prompting, the proposed agent will identify the requested creative mode, retrieve relevant regional vocabulary and cultural references from a curated knowledge base, and generate text adapted to the user's intent. An MCP server will expose tools such as a syllable counter and rhyme checker, allowing the agent to evaluate its output and make revisions when necessary.The use of RAG will help ground generation in relevant regional expressions, folk traditions, and stylistic examples. Additionally, we will investigate fine-tuning an open-source SLM on a curated dataset of Romanian regional poetry and rap lyrics to improve stylistic consistency, depending on data availability and implementation feasibility.The main advantage of our approach is the combination of **cultural grounding, flexible generation, and tool-assisted structural validation**. Rather than relying exclusively on fixed templates or manually engineered prompts, the system will explore how retrieval and agent-driven tool use can produce more relevant, regionally expressive, and structurally consistent creative text.
